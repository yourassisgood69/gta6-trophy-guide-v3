Roadmap to the platinum

Play in this order. Story first, then strangers, then activities, then collectibles, and the combat and money trophies last. Only three trophies can be missed, and all three are flagged below.

    Missable trophies — only 3

    A trophy is flagged missable only when story progress or a one-time choice can lock it out for good. Finishing a story mission is never missable, because missions can be replayed, and a repeatable open-world feat is never missable either.

    Where the facts come from

    Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags follow the official PlayStation 5 list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every name, description and grade. Counts such as 42 Shooting Range challenges or 18 meditation spots come from PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in our own words from the official descriptions, and each one names its sources beside it. Where a method is not yet confirmed, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
