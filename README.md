An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide in our own words that names its sources, a roadmap to the platinum, and just three missable trophies flagged — only the ones a story choice or a one-time moment can lock out. Progress saves in the browser, with export and import, filters by grade and kind, search, and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned. The app is plain static files and works with the network off.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
